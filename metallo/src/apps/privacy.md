# Privacy Policy

*Last updated: 31 March 2026*

This policy applies to all apps published by Mokacoding Pty Ltd.

## Data Collection

Our apps do **not** collect, transmit, or share any personal data.
All your data is stored locally on your device.
Nothing is sent to our servers or any third party.

## Analytics

Our apps do not include any analytics or tracking frameworks.

## In-App Purchases

Some of our apps offer optional in-app purchases.
These transactions are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store.
We do not have access to your payment information.
